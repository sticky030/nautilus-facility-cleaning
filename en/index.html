Move-out cleaning

How much does a move-out cleaning cost in Berlin?

A move-out cleaning (Auszugsreinigung or Endreinigung) with Nautilus Facility Cleaning costs from 155 € for a broom-clean 1-room flat and 217 € handover-ready. Handover-ready covers what German rental contracts usually mean by a cleaned flat. Book the cleaning one day before the handover, so there is time if you notice anything on your own walk-through.

Moving out

Moving out of a rented flat in Berlin?

Most German rental contracts require the flat to be returned clean. At the handover (Wohnungsübergabe) landlords and property managers usually check kitchen, bathroom, windows, floors and radiators. Nautilus Facility Cleaning cleans your flat to the handover-ready standard, ideally the day before, so you can walk into the handover relaxed.

  • Send us the cleaning clause of your contract or a photo of it. We tell you in English which standard fits.
  • Send photos of the flat by WhatsApp. You get a binding fixed price before we start.
  • You do not have to be there during the cleaning. How we get the keys is agreed with you in advance.
  • Re-cleaning free of charge: if you notice something within our agreed scope on your own walk-through, we come back.
  • Deposit (Kaution): the decision is up to your landlord, but a properly cleaned flat removes one of the most common reasons for deductions.

Moving out of Berlin or abroad? Write to us early, we plan the date around your handover.
